### Summary
UKG CIO Prakash Kota describes moving AI experiments into production through shared intake, business ownership, workflow integration, and short feedback cycles. His account offers a practical approach to coordinating pilots and evaluating adoption, though the interview provides no quantified evidence of business returns.

### Key Insights
- Kota says duplicate experiments with identical vendors prompted a shared AI hub. Anyone can submit ideas; functional champions prioritize business outcomes instead of routing every decision through a central team.
- UKG’s portfolio separates scaling, growth, and exploration. Kota describes iterations capped at 90 days, user feedback, and demo days that share both successful deployments and experiments archived for possible reconsideration.
- Kota reports that standalone agents lost adoption when users returned to their regular systems. Workflow integration matters, but support experiments also revealed a qualification: customers facing emergencies wanted immediate human access.
- UKG shifted measurement from prompts and generated code toward workflow use and validated code reaching production. These indicate operational uptake; the interview does not establish how much business value they produced.

### Actions
- [ ] Inventory pilots by vendor, capability, and owner; combine overlapping experiments to reduce duplicated effort and share learning.
- [ ] Select one workflow and record its baseline outcome; run a pilot within 90 days to validate whether AI improves effectiveness and speed.
- [ ] Define urgent support scenarios and human routing before testing agents; validate customer feedback to preserve access to help when users are stressed.

### Implementation Prompts
> Design a pilot charter for [workflow]. Specify its business outcome, baseline, functional champion, existing approved tools, workflow integration, and an iteration lasting no more than 90 days. Include user feedback, relevant human escalation, and criteria to scale, revise, or archive. Distinguish usage metrics from evidence of business value.
